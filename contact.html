How to Reach Habarana, Sri Lanka

Strategically situated at the central junction of Sri Lanka's cultural and wildlife wonders

Approximately 165 km (3.5 to 4 hours) via the Central Expressway (E04) and Kurunegala / Dambulla Highway.

Only 15 km (20 minutes) from Sigiriya Rock Fortress and Pidurangala rock viewpoint.
