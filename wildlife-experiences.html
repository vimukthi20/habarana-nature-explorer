WILDLIFE EXPERIENCES

Discover the wild side of Habarana

Habarana is a wonderful starting point for exploring wildlife and nature in Sri Lanka's Cultural Triangle region. Visitors can learn about elephants, observe wildlife in suitable natural habitats and enjoy guided wildlife experiences. Our aim is to encourage responsible wildlife tourism while respecting animals and protecting their natural environments.

Habarana Wildlife Programs

Select an experience to explore full details and check availability

Experience 01

1. Wildlife Safari Experiences

Explore the natural landscapes around Habarana and learn about Sri Lanka's wildlife through suitable guided safari experiences. Observe animals from a safe and respectful distance.

Experience 02

2. Elephant Viewing

Learn about Sri Lankan elephants and their natural behaviour. Wildlife viewing opportunities depend on animal movements, environmental conditions and the location visited.

Experience 03

3. Guided Wildlife Observation

Discover animal behaviour, habitats and the importance of biodiversity with a knowledgeable guide. Enjoy observing wildlife without disturbing the animals.

Experience 04

4. Responsible Wildlife Tourism

Respect wildlife by maintaining a safe distance, avoiding feeding animals and following park rules. Responsible tourism helps protect wildlife and natural habitats.

Important Wildlife Notice

Wildlife sightings are not guaranteed. Animals move freely in their natural habitats, and sightings depend on weather, season and other environmental conditions. Visitors should follow their guide's instructions and respect wildlife at all times.

Take the Habarana Ethical Tourism Pledge

Support conservation efforts by pledging never to crowd elephants, litter in national parks, or purchase unauthorized wildlife products.
