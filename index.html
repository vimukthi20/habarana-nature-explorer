Welcome to Habarana Nature Explorer

Discover Nature. Experience Wildlife. Explore Habarana.

Welcome to Habarana Nature Explorer, your guide to discovering the natural beauty and wildlife experiences of Habarana, Sri Lanka. Surrounded by lush greenery and rich natural landscapes, Habarana is an ideal starting point for exploring Sri Lanka's wildlife and nature. From exciting safari experiences to peaceful birdwatching and guided nature walks, there is something for every nature lover.

Our aim is to inspire visitors to explore nature, appreciate wildlife and experience the beauty of Sri Lanka responsibly. Explore the wild side of Sri Lanka with us!
